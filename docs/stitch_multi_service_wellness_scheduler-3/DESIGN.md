## Brand & Style

This design system embodies mindful tranquility, grounding presence, and quiet luxury. Crafted for a boutique somatic practice and holistic wellness studio, the aesthetic prioritizes nervous-system regulation: deliberate pauses, spaciousness, and organic tactile warmth.

The visual narrative blends **Minimalism** with subtle **Tactile Earthiness**. By rejecting stark clinical white and jarring contrasts, interfaces feel like stepping into a sunlit studio with limestone plaster walls, linen textures, and steamed botanical teas. Interactions are gentle, unhurried, and grounded, evoking grounded presence, reassurance, and embodied balance for seekers of restorative bodywork, mindful movement, and intentional living.

## Colors

The color palette translates raw botanical and mineral pigments into digital surfaces:

- **Primary (`#4A6151` - Deep Moss Sage):** Represents stability, restorative growth, and nature. Applied to focal calls-to-action, active indicators, and prominent branding anchors.
- **Secondary (`#BD6B4B` - Warm Terracotta/Clay):** An earthy, human warmth reminiscent of fired earthenware. Used sparingly for highlights, workshop dates, somatic focus tags, and subtle accent details.
- **Tertiary (`#768A7E` - Soft Heather Sage):** An ambient botanical mid-tone utilized for secondary actions, non-critical icons, and calm framing elements.
- **Neutral (`#F4EFEA` - Oatmeal/Linen Sand):** The breathing canvas of the studio. Replaces harsh digital white with an organic, gentle foundation that softens optical fatigue.
- **Ink & Contrast Support:** Text is anchored in `#242B26` (Charcoal Slate Pine), ensuring crisp WCAG AAA legibility while eschewing severe pure black. Surface card layers utilize a pristine warm alabaster (`#FAF7F2`) lifted against the canvas.

## Typography

The typographical cadence balances literary contemplation with functional clarity:

- **Headings (`Newsreader`):** An editorial, high-character serif that feels cultivated, poetic, and grounded. Its gentle stroke terminals and italic cuts bring artisanal authenticity to workshop titles, practitioner reflections, and intentional prompts.
- **Body & Labels (`Manrope`):** A modern, geometric sans-serif offering neutral, open counter-shapes and balanced proportions. It ensures effortless comprehension for booking flows, intake assessments, and practice schedules without competing with the display serif.
- **Atmospheric Stylings:** Key practitioner quotes and somatic mantras utilize `Newsreader` italic styling with generous vertical leading (`lineHeight` exceeding `1.5x`), giving words ambient room to resonate.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to a serene, structured grid that rejects visual density in favor of intentional negative space:

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views operate on a 12-column fluid grid capped at a centered maximum width of `1280px`. Tablet transitions to an 8-column layout, while mobile relies on a single or 4-column flow.
- **Rhythm & Pace:** Vertical page spacing intentionally expands between content blocks. Sections are separated by generous `space-xl` intervals to induce visual calm and deliberate scrolling.
- **Reflow Rules:** Complex multi-column scheduling timetables and multi-step intake cards collapse into linear stacked journeys on touch viewports, retaining full touch targets (`min-height: 48px`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Layers** combined with **Ambient Botanical Shadows** rather than rigid structural borders:

- **Surface Layering:** The primary backdrop is the oatmeal canvas (`#F4EFEA`). Elevated elements (treatment cards, modal dialogues, session drawers) use a warmer tinted alabaster (`#FAF7F2`), naturally rising above the ground without harsh line work.
- **Ambient Diffusion:** Shadows mimic warm, filtered afternoon light passing through rice paper. They possess wide radii, low opacity, and are tinted with deep earth tones rather than cold neutral gray:
  - *Resting card shadow:* `0 4px 24px -2px rgba(54, 48, 41, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(54, 48, 41, 0.02)`
  - *Floating modal / Popover shadow:* `0 16px 48px -4px rgba(45, 40, 34, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(45, 40, 34, 0.03)`
- **Low-Contrast Separation:** When crisp delineation is required, single-pixel hairline dividers tinted in `#E5DDD4` provide structure without interrupting the flow of consciousness.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates soft, organic geometries inspired by river stones, ceramic bowls, and human forms:

- **Containers & Surface Enclosures:** Interactive cards, intake forms, and image containers embrace balanced curvatures (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`), generating an inviting, tactile enclosure.
- **Floating Controls & Micro-Elements:** Quick filters, duration badges, and status labels utilize pill shapes (`9999px`) to echo physical stones and smooth seeds.
- **Visual Harmony:** Sharp 90-degree corners are systematically avoided across all structural modules to maintain a gentle, unthreatening visual rhythm.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Buttons:** Deep Moss Green (`#4A6151`) fill with warm ivory typography (`#FAF7F2`). Border-radius is gently rounded (`rounded-md` or `0.5rem`). Hover states shift subtly to a deeper pine tone (`#3D5043`) with a soft tactile translateY transition (`-1px`).
- **Secondary Buttons:** Transparent fill wrapped in a low-contrast bone-clay outline (`#D4C9BC`), adopting an ethereal moss tint on hover.
- **Ghost/Tertiary Links:** Minimal text links styled in `Newsreader` italic or `Manrope` uppercase labels, underlined with an intentional 1px terracotta stroke offset by 4px.

### Duration & State Pills
- Compact indicators designed for session lengths (e.g., "75 min", "90 min somatic release").
- Rendered in pill borders (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.375rem 0.875rem`, featuring a soft sage-tinted ground (`rgba(118, 138, 126, 0.12)`) and deep moss text. Active or selected pills shift into solid terracotta (`#BD6B4B`) with white text.

### Interactive Treatment Cards
- Finished in warm alabaster (`#FAF7F2`) with 16px internal padding and ambient drop shadows.
- Include a subtle top hairline accent in terracotta or moss upon interactive focus. Content hierarchy presents the session modality in `Newsreader`, duration/modality pills grouped cleanly at the top, followed by a succinct contemplative description.

### Mindful Timeline & Journey Visualizations
- Used for session flows, somatic unfolding stages, or workshop itineraries.
- Features a central or left-aligned 1.5px vertical line in warm clay-beige (`#E0D7CC`).
- Milestones are marked by hollow circular river-stone nodes (`12px`) that fill with moss green upon active completion. Accompanying timestamps and phase descriptions alternate with ample vertical breath.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Inputs:** Understated fields framed with a soft border (`#D8CEBF`), tinted with an ultra-light oat background. On active focus, transitions smoothly to a deep moss border with zero harsh outer glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved organic corners (`rounded-sm` for checkboxes, circular for radios). Checked states display deep moss fill with a soft clay or ivory check glyph, eliminating clinical blue or harsh standard web controls.

### Meditative Modals & Session Drawers
- Off-canvas booking drawers slide out like linen screens, accompanied by a diffused oatmeal backdrop overlay (`rgba(36, 43, 38, 0.35)` with an 8px backdrop blur).
